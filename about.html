Meet Azande Madlala

Azande Madlala is the braiding specialist behind Azande Hair & Beauty, a hair braiding studio based in Sandton, Gauteng.

Her braiding journey began in Durban, where she developed her skills and built relationships with clients who appreciate carefully styled hair.

Today, Azande specialises in a range of hairstyles, including Lemonade Braids, Cornrows, Free Hand Styles, Straight Up Braids and Pondo Braids.

Her approach focuses on neat finishes, attention to detail and helping clients choose styles that suit their preferences.

At Azande Hair & Beauty, every appointment is an opportunity to create a hairstyle that helps clients feel confident and beautiful.
