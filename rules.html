Salon Rules & Policies

Please review our salon rules and booking policies before scheduling your appointment. These guidelines help us provide a smooth, professional and enjoyable experience for everyone.

content_cut

Wash & Blow-Dry Required

Please wash and blow-dry your hair before coming to your appointment. This prepares your hair for braiding and ensures the best results.

confirmation_number

Confirm Your Booking

Please confirm your booking the day before your appointment. Unconfirmed bookings may be released to other clients.

cancel

Early Cancellation

If you wish to cancel your appointment, please notify us as early as possible. Early cancellation allows another client to book the available appointment slot.

Last-minute cancellations may affect our ability to accommodate other customers.

payments

Pricing Policy

Hairstyle prices do not include hairpieces unless specifically stated in the hairstyle description.

Please contact us before your appointment if you would like assistance purchasing hairpieces.

Final pricing should be confirmed before your appointment.

View available hairstyles
location_on

House Call Policy

House call services are available around Durban, including Berea.

An additional transport fee applies to house call appointments.

Please note that taxi transportation is not used for house calls within Durban.

Contact us to confirm house call availability and the applicable transport fee.

access_time

Late Arrival

Please arrive on time for your scheduled appointment.

Late arrivals may result in a shortened appointment or rescheduling to avoid affecting other clients.

If you expect to arrive late, please contact us as soon as possible.

weekend

Weekend Appointments

Saturday and Sunday services are available by appointment only.

Walk-in customers are welcome Monday through Friday at the Sandton studio.

We recommend contacting us before visiting to confirm availability.

info

By booking an appointment with Azande Hair & Beauty, you acknowledge and agree to the salon rules and policies listed above.
