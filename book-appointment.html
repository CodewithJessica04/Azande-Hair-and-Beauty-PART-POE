Book an Appointment

Choose your preferred hairstyle, date and time, then send your booking request through WhatsApp.

Complete the form below. Your booking details will open in WhatsApp for you to review and send. Your appointment is only confirmed after Azande responds and confirms availability.

House calls are available in Durban's Berea area only. Transport fees apply.
Some hairstyles include a hairpiece. Confirm the final price before your appointment.

By requesting an appointment, you acknowledge our salon rules and cancellation policy. Please wash and blow-dry your hair before arriving.

Important: Opening WhatsApp does not automatically send your request. You must send the prepared message and wait for confirmation.
